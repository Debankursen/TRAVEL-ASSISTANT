# TRAVEL-ASSISTANT
Developed a Travel Assistant system designed to support travellers by providing accurate travel-related information. The system assists users with destination details, travel guidance, and general customer support queries.Focused on improving user experience, quick information access, and reliable assistance for Travellers.

# PROBLEM STATEMENT
Focus on the challenges travelers face when planning and managing trips, including 
the time and effort involved in research, booking, and handling unexpected changes.
A. Lack of personalized recommendations: generic travel websites and search 
engines may not always provide the most suitable options based on individual 
preferences and travel styles. 
B. Time-consuming research and booking: finding the best deals, comparing different 
options, and coordinating all aspects of the trip can be time-intensive.
